✓
Every pinout here was checked against two independent sources. STM32 comes from ST's own STM32_open_pin_data database, cross-checked against the KiCad symbol library and, where the PDF converted cleanly, the datasheet itself. AVR, PIC and Nordic parts come from the official vendor datasheet cross-checked pin-for-pin against KiCad. Each device shows its sources below the selectors. Nothing is listed that failed the check — but check the datasheet yourself before you cut a board.
